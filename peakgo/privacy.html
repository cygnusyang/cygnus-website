PeakGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Applies to version 1.0 and later · Platform: iOS

In one sentence: PeakGo collects no personal data. Altitude, pressure, heading and coordinates are read from on-device sensors and computed on device, never uploaded. No account, no cloud sync, no ads, no third-party analytics SDK, no tracking.

3 · Subscriptions and payment

  • Subscriptions are handled by the App Store (Apple). We do not receive your name, card number, billing address or Apple ID email.
  • The app uses Apple's StoreKit framework to load product information and verify purchase receipts. These requests are made by Apple's system components; we cannot identify you from them.
  • We only read one on-device conclusion: whether a valid subscription is currently active. That status contains no personal information.
  • Refunds, cancellation and purchase history are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com or in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Subscription processing is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

4 · Network access

The app contains no network code of its own: no backend, no upload endpoint, no remote configuration. The only network activity comes from Apple's system components for the subscription flow described above, and from system services such as app updates and crash reporting.

If you enable sharing in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash reports and usage statistics. That data is collected and controlled by Apple; no collection code ships in the app and we cannot identify any individual from it. You can turn sharing off at any time.

5 · Retention and deletion

  • Preferences (units, language, theme, calibration baseline) and pressure/altitude history are stored in the app's local sandbox.
  • Deleting the app deletes them. We keep no copy and cannot restore or remove them remotely.
  • Because no server-side data exists, there is no remote data on which a deletion request could operate.

6 · Children

The app has no ads, no paid content beyond the optional subscription, and collects no data, so it is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; in fact we collect no one's personal information.

7 · Your rights

Under the EU GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA, China's Personal Information Protection Law and other applicable laws you have rights of access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent and portability. Because the app collects no personal information, transfers none across borders and makes no automated decisions, these rights have no corresponding processing activity to act on. If you have questions or complaints, contact us at the address below; we reply within 15 business days.

8 · Third-party links

The in-app "Terms of Use" link opens Apple's Standard End User License Agreement; the "Privacy Policy" link returns to this page. No other third-party sites are opened.

9 · Changes

If a future version introduces features that need network access or involve data processing (for example optional cloud backup), we will update this policy before that feature ships and notify you prominently in the app. Each revision changes the "Last updated" date above; significant changes require your confirmation in the app before taking effect.
