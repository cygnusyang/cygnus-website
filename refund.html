Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

14-day no-questions-asked refund for KaidoPlan purchases made through Paddle. Whatever the reason, request a refund within 14 days of payment and we refund in full. No explanation required.

1. Scope

Where you boughtWho processes the refund
KaidoPlan, purchased through Paddle (direct download)Paddle, as Merchant of Record, back to your original payment method
BitcofferNo online checkout. If a paid licence was issued by email, contact ruohuyang@163.com

2. How to request

For a KaidoPlan purchase made through Paddle:

  1. Email ruohuyang@163.com with your order ID or the email used at purchase; or
  2. Contact Paddle buyer support directly with your order ID.

Once confirmed, Paddle issues the refund back to your original payment method.

Bitcoffer currently has no online checkout and no Paddle orders. If a paid licence was issued by email, write to ruohuyang@163.com to request a refund.

3. Timing

For KaidoPlan refunds issued by Paddle, funds typically reach your original payment method within 5–10 business days after Paddle issues them, depending on your card issuer or payment channel. Cross-border and some local payment methods take longer; that part is outside our control.

4. After a refund

For a refunded KaidoPlan purchase, the licence is revoked — its license.json stops working. Your project files are untouched and remain usable with the free feature set. If a paid Bitcoffer licence issued by email is refunded, that licence is revoked; your vault stays on the device and remains readable and exportable.

5. Renewals

KaidoPlan annual subscriptions purchased through Paddle do not renew-refund automatically. If you request a refund within 14 days of a renewal charge, this policy applies. If you no longer use the app, cancel before the renewal date.
